FAQ

Questions before you spar?

Is this a real phone call?

It's a simulated practice call with an AI buyer, designed to feel like the pressure of a live sales conversation. You talk out loud, the AI responds in real time.

Is Sales Sparring available now?

Yes — Sales Sparring is live on the App Store for iOS. Download it free and start practicing immediately.

Can teams use this?

The current version is built for individual reps. Team workflows will come after launch based on demand. Email us if you're interested in team plans.

Does this replace sales coaching?

No. It gives reps more repetitions between coaching sessions so coaching time gets more useful. Think of it as a batting cage for sales calls.

What languages does it support?

Currently English. The AI buyer and coaching feedback are optimized for English sales conversations.
